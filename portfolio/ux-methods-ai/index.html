AI Process

The Challenge

For many projects, there's a need to get up to speed in a new space very quickly. Traditionally, I would spend lots of time and effort reading and creating mental models of the current research and documentation so I can have an informed point-of-view that I would use to make decisions about strategy and design.


Another thing that can be really time consuming is crafting a solid storyboard to aid in telling a product or user story. When you have the luxury of time, digging into these using traditional methods is great, but there's a new way. Enter AI.

AI-Powered Literature Reviews


Tool: NotebookLM

AI has revolutionized my literature review process, enabling me to:


  • Quickly identify the most relevant research
  • Minimize the risk of hallucinations by sticking to specific sources
  • Create diverse content types (briefing documents, FAQs, audio overviews) tailored to different learning styles
  • Efficiently extract insights from multiple sources simultaneously

AI-Powered Principles


Tool: NotebookLM

I use a principles framework to generate product principles. Traditionally, I'll do a workshop activity with xfn partners to generate and align on these principles based on the current available research. The alignment exercise is the most valuable piece since your team has to be aligned on what "good" means as you're making decisions.


Instead of doing a full workshop, I run the workshop steps through NotebookLM to generate product principles in seconds. Raw research insights are created based on sources, then turned into postulates in the categories of user context, user needs, and technological constraints. From these you can identify patterns that can be distilled into principles.


This allows principles to be dynamic as the project progresses — as you add new research and literature you can update the principles. I would argue that using AI here can help you be more objective from the start since the postulates are created in a more automated fashion from the source material.

Filling Gaps in Strategic Plans


Tool: NotebookLM

AI has helped me more quickly drive strategic change by:


  • Cross-referencing program/project strategy docs with organization-level objectives to create suggestions for better alignment
  • Cross-referencing program/project strategy with the current research to uncover where our strategy might conflict with what we know
  • Creating multi-modal overviews to help me more quickly internalize the strategy
