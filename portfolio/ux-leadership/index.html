Lessons learned

Let's just say the lessons were plentiful. Managing a team within a large platform organization means navigating significant organizational complexity — competing priorities, unclear ownership, and the challenge of driving design quality when eng bandwidth is always constrained. The most important lesson: be explicit about what "done" means before you start, not after.
